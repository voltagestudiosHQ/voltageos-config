# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve voltage!os!

## Contributions and usage rights
By submitting a contribution to this repository, you give voltage!studios the right to use, copy, modify, publish, distribute, and otherwise use your contribution in any way, without restriction or payment. Please only submit work you have the right to contribute.

## For voltage!studios workers
- Do not make changes directly on `main`.
- Create a separate branch for your work.
- Open a pull request (PR) against `main` and get it reviewed before merging.
- Keep PRs focused and describe what you changed and why.
